# Parcel Board

A small board in the corner of the screen that tells you how many parcels are in the level and where they go.

## What it does

- **One small table.** A column for all parcels and one per mark: Heavy, Fragile, Dark, Frozen, Hot and the rest.
  A row per destination, Cat's Island first, those are the ones that arrived. Everything under the line is to send.
- **Damaged** parcels that need the repair table, and parcels **still to stamp**.
- **Sizes.** How many parcels of each size there are, with a picture of the box and the shelf cells it takes.
- Closed, it shows only the totals; **Ctrl+O** opens the rows. **Ctrl+P** hides the whole board.
  It steps aside by itself when the pause menu or the day recap is open.

## Settings

Settings → Mods → Parcel Board.

- Which lists to show: always, only when they have parcels, or never.
- Hide destinations with no parcels, or keep them.
- The corner of the screen, the size, how see-through the background is.
- Destination names next to their stamps, or just the stamps.
- Count everything, or leave out the parcels on the boat and at customers.

## Playing with friends

Only you need it. It works with any host, with or without mods.
