# Stack it!

Two 3×2 parcels side by side make a flat top, but the game won't let you put a 2×2 parcel in the middle of it, across the joint.
On the boat it would stand there just fine. This mod lets it stand on the shelves and on top of other parcels too.

## What it does

- **Across joints.** A parcel can stand on two or more parcels at once when their tops are level and it lies fully on them.
  A 5×2 parcel fits on two Standards and a StandardLong, a Giant on two Mediums side by side.
- **No hanging in the air.** Every part of it has to lie on a parcel, all in the same storage.
- **Take one away and it falls.** Pull a parcel from under it and it slides off to that side and falls. Fragile ones may not survive the landing.
- **Marks still count.** A parcel across a fragile one breaks it, a heavy one damages everything under it, like in the game.

## Settings

Settings → Mods → Stack it!. The host's settings count for everyone.

- **Stand across joints**: on or off.
- **Keep balance**: instead of falling, the parcel stays while its centre is still above a parcel.

## Playing with friends

Everyone needs it: the host and every player.

## Saves

Saves stay safe. A save made with the mod loads without it, the parcel then just stands on the parcel under its centre.
