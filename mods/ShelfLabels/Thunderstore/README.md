# Shelf Labels

More than one label per shelf, so one shelf can say both "Port Windy" and "Fragile".

## How to use

- Up to three extra labels appear next to every shelf label. They work like the game's own:
  left click for the next picture, right click for the previous one.
- Some shelves have no room on one side. Look at a label and press **Ctrl+L** to move its extra labels:
  to the freer side, right, left, below, or none at all for that shelf.
- The wooden stand under a label doesn't fit every shelf. Look at a label and press **Ctrl+K** to hide or show
  the stands of its extra labels.

Your labels are kept with your save. Remove the mod and the save is just as before; put it back and the labels return.

## Settings

Settings → Mods → Shelf Labels.

- **Extra labels:** how many each shelf gets, from 0 to 3.
- **Placement:** where extra labels go on shelves you haven't set yourself.
- **Hide stands:** stands on or off for shelves you haven't set yourself.
- **Gap:** space between the labels.
- **Change placement** and **Stand on or off:** the two keys, Ctrl+L and Ctrl+K by default.

## Playing with friends

Everyone in the game needs Shelf Labels. Anyone can change the labels, and everyone sees the same.
